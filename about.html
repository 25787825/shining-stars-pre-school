Message from the Principal

Partnering With Families During the Most Formative Years

Dear Parents, welcome to Shining Stars! The years between ages two and twelve lay the lifelong foundation for how a child views learning, friendships, and their own voice in the world.

Our educators combine gentle encouragement with purposeful structure. In our Preschool Wing, children explore at their own joyful pace with Montessori materials. In our Primary Academy, they channel that natural curiosity into strong reading, problem-solving mathematics, scientific inquiry, and compassionate leadership.

I warmly invite you to visit our campus, meet our teachers, and see our classrooms in action.

Iqra Azeem
Founder - SSP Speech & Hope | Certified Global Montessori Educator, ECE Specialist

Our Guiding Compass

The Shining S.T.A.R. Values

Every classroom agreement, morning assembly, and student recognition celebrates four timeless habits.

S

Safety & Kindness

Creating emotionally and physically safe classrooms where every child feels heard and respected.

T

Trust & Honesty

Building open partnerships between parents, teachers, and students rooted in integrity.

A

Aspiration & Wonder

Encouraging joyful questions, brave attempts, and pride in thoughtful work.

R

Resilience

Teaching children that mistakes are stepping stones and effort turns challenges into mastery.

Campus Care

Safety & Wellbeing Promise

Parents trust us with what matters most. Here is how we protect every child every day.

Verified Gate & Pick-Up

Controlled main entry, parent ID cards for authorized pick-up, and continuous perimeter CCTV monitoring.

Nut-Aware & Hygienic Campus

Sanitized classroom toys daily, allergy-conscious dining rooms, and filtered drinking water stations.

Pediatric First Aid Ready

On-site infirmary staffed by a full-time nurse, plus CPR/First-Aid certified classroom teachers.
