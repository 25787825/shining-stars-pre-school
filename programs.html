Programs Crafted for Every Stage (Ages 2–12)

From a toddler’s first joyful circle time to Grade 5 science inquiry and robotics, every year builds smoothly upon the last.

Ages 2.0 – 3.0 Years • Ratio 8:1

Playgroup — Sensory Discovery

A warm, unhurried welcome to school life designed around tactile play, joyful rhythm, language immersion, and secure emotional bonding.

  • Sensory bins, finger painting, and Montessori pouring/sorting trays
  • Daily music, movement, rhymes, and picture-book read-alouds
  • Gentle social routines: sharing, self-help, and outdoor garden play

Timings: 8:30 AM – 12:30 PM (Optional Stay & Play until 2:30 PM)

Inquire for Playgroup
Ages 3.0 – 4.0 Years • Ratio 10:1

Nursery — Wonder & Early Phonics

Children begin connecting sounds to letters, patterns to numbers, and curiosity to questions through hands-on Montessori and thematic centers.

  • Jolly Phonics sound games, vocabulary cards, and storytelling circles
  • Tactile number rods, pattern blocks, and sorting by attributes
  • Nature walks, dramatic role-play corners, and fine-motor studio

Timings: 8:30 AM – 12:30 PM (After-School Care available)

Inquire for Nursery
Ages 4.0 – 6.0 Years • Ratio 12:1

Kindergarten (KG I & II) — School Readiness

A joyful bridge to primary academics where young learners gain fluency in reading, expressive writing, early addition/subtraction, and scientific curiosity.

  • Guided reading groups, sight-word journals, and show-and-tell speaking
  • Concrete-to-pictorial numeracy: place value, time, coins, and measurement
  • Weekly kitchen science, world cultures, and introductory coding games

Timings: 8:15 AM – 1:15 PM (Extended Care until 4:30 PM)

Inquire for Kindergarten
Grades 1 – 5 (Ages 6 – 12) • Ratio 15:1

Primary Academy — Inquiry & Mastery

A rich elementary curriculum balancing core academic depth with project-based STEM, critical thinking, public speaking, and character leadership.

  • Core Subjects: English Language & Literature, Conceptual Math, Inquiry Science, Social Studies
  • Future Skills: Weekly Robotics & Coding Lab, Library Research, Presentation Skills
  • Balanced Growth: Visual Arts, Music Ensemble, Athletics & Character Education

Timings: 8:00 AM – 2:15 PM (Clubs & Homework Lounge until 4:30 PM)

Grade-by-Grade Academic Focus (Grades 1–5)

Every primary grade integrates literacy, problem-solving math, lab science, and creative arts.

Grade Level Literacy & Humanities Math & Logic Science & Tech Signature Project
